Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Structured for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)

⚠️ Template status: this policy is a working draft prepared for legal review. It becomes binding only after finalization by counsel and formal publication. Borderless Pay currently operates in sandbox settlement mode — no real money moves and no real KYC documents are collected until licensed rails and a KYC vendor are live.

1. Who we are

Borderless Pay ("we") provides a payments application for domestic (India) and cross-border payments. For personal data collected through the app, we act as the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act, 2023.

Device permissions are requested only when a feature genuinely needs them, at the moment of use.

3. How we protect it

Field-level AES-256-GCM encryption at rest, scrypt hashing for secrets, TLS in transit, tamper-evident hash-chained ledgers, device-bound sessions, and strict access controls. Our security posture is documented publicly in the project's security documentation.

4. Retention

Profile data is kept while your account is active. If you close your account, profile data is erased; transaction records are retained pseudonymously where law requires (PMLA record-retention when live operations begin).

5. Your rights (DPDP Act)

  • Access: view your transaction history in-app at any time, and download a machine-readable copy of everything we hold about you — self-serve in the Privacy center or in the app under Help → Privacy & your data (API: POST /api/account/export, password required).
  • Correction: correct your name or country yourself in the Privacy center or in the app under Help → Privacy & your data (API: POST /api/account/profile); corrections re-run the sanctions/KYC check against your corrected identity.
  • Erasure / consent withdrawal: close your account in-app (Profile → Close account) or via POST /api/account/close; profile data is erased immediately and all sessions revoked.
  • Grievance: Grievance Officer — [name and contact to be appointed before public launch]. You may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Data location

Payment-system data is stored in India, in line with RBI data-localisation requirements.

7. Changes

Policy versions are numbered. If this policy changes materially, you will be asked to review and re-accept before continuing to use the app.

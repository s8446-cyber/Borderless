Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Template pending final legal review · Aligned with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)

1. Who we are

Borderless Pay ("we") provides a payments application for domestic (India) and cross-border payments. For personal data collected through the app, we act as the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act, 2023.

3. What we do NOT collect

Camera: used only while you scan a payment QR, with your permission asked at that moment; the QR is decoded on your device and no photo or video is ever captured, stored, or uploaded. We do not collect: your contacts, location, microphone audio, photos, advertising identifiers, or any data unrelated to payments. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising. Device permissions are requested only when a feature genuinely needs them, at the moment of use.

4. How we protect it

Field-level AES-256-GCM encryption at rest, scrypt hashing for secrets, TLS in transit, tamper-evident hash-chained ledgers, device-bound sessions, and strict access controls. Our security posture is documented publicly in the project's security documentation.

5. Consent

Account creation requires your explicit, versioned consent to this policy and the Terms of Service. We record which version you accepted and when, in a tamper-evident audit log. You may withdraw consent at any time by closing your account.

6. Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

  • Access: request a summary of your personal data we hold.
  • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate personal data.
  • Erasure / consent withdrawal: close your account in-app (Profile → Close account) or via POST /api/account/close; profile data is erased immediately and all sessions revoked.
  • Grievance redressal: contact our grievance officer (below); escalation to the Data Protection Board of India is your statutory right.
  • Nominate: nominate a person to exercise these rights in case of death or incapacity.

7. Retention

Profile data lives only while your account is open. When you close your account, profile PII is erased immediately. Transaction records are retained pseudonymously (an internal identifier only — no name, email, or bank reference) because payment-systems law (PMLA/RBI record-keeping) requires it; the hash-chained ledger is append-only by design and cannot be silently rewritten.

8. Sharing

We share data only with processors strictly necessary to run the service (e.g., a licensed KYC provider during verification, a PSP/sponsor bank for settlement — once live rails launch), each bound by contract to this policy's standards. We never sell personal data.

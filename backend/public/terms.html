1. The service (current phase)

Borderless Pay is presently a pre-production demonstration: onboarding, payments, receipts, and ledger verification operate on simulated rails with demo balances. Nothing in the app constitutes a real payment, financial product, remittance, or investment.

2. Eligibility & your account

  • You must be 18+ and provide accurate information.

3. Fees & rates (as designed)

Domestic payments: ₹0 fee. Cross-border: the real mid-market exchange rate with zero FX markup plus a flat 0.5% fee (₹2 minimum, ₹500 maximum). Every fee and rate is shown before you confirm.

4. Receipts & the ledger

Transactions are recorded on a tamper-evident, hash-chained ledger. Receipts include cryptographic material (settlement hash, anchor, signature) that anyone can verify. Ledger records are append-only and cannot be altered retroactively — including by us.
